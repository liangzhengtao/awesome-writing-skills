# SEO Blog Post Writing

> Write blog posts that rank on search engines and engage readers.

## When to Use

- Writing a new blog post targeting specific keywords
- Optimizing existing content for better search rankings
- Creating pillar content or cornerstone articles
- Building topical authority in your niche
- Producing how-to guides, listicles, or comparison posts

---

## Writing Framework

### Step 1: Keyword Research

Before writing a single word, identify your target keywords.

| Element | Tool/Method | Goal |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Primary keyword | Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Ubersuggest | 1 main topic |
| Secondary keywords | Related searches, "People also ask" | 3-5 supporting terms |
| Long-tail keywords | AnswerThePublic, Google Autocomplete | 5-10 specific phrases |
| Search intent | Analyze top 10 results | Match user intent |

**Search Intent Types:**
- **Informational** — "how to," "what is," "guide to"
- **Navigational** — brand or product names
- **Transactional** — "buy," "pricing," "best [product]"
- **Commercial investigation** — "vs," "review," "comparison"

### Step 2: Headline Formulas

Use these proven headline structures:

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| How to [Achieve Result] Without [Pain Point] | How to Double Your Traffic Without Paid Ads |
| [Number] [Adjective] Ways to [Achieve Result] | 17 Proven Ways to Boost Email Open Rates |
| The Ultimate Guide to [Topic] in [Year] | The Ultimate Guide to Content Marketing in 2025 |
| [Topic]: [Promise/Benefit] | SEO Writing: How to Rank #1 in 90 Days |
| Why [Common Belief] Is Wrong (And What to Do Instead) | Why Daily Blogging Is Wrong (And What to Do Instead) |
| [Do This] to [Achieve Result] — [Proof/Authority] | Write Headlines That Convert — Backed by 10M Data Points |

### Step 3: Meta Description

Write a compelling meta description (150-160 characters):

```
Formula: [Hook] + [Value proposition] + [CTA]

Example: "Learn 15 proven SEO writing techniques that helped us increase organic
traffic by 340%. Step-by-step framework with templates included. Read now."
```

### Step 4: Content Structure

```
1. H1 Title (include primary keyword)
   │
2. Introduction
   ├── Hook (statistic, question, or story)
   ├── Problem statement
   └── Promise (what the reader will learn)
   │
3. Body (H2/H3 sections)
   ├── Each section = one subtopic
   ├── Include secondary keywords naturally
   ├── Use bullet points, tables, and images
   └── Internal links to related content
   │
4. Conclusion
   ├── Key takeaways (summary)
   ├── Actionable next steps
   └── CTA (comment, share, subscribe)
   │
5. FAQ Section (for featured snippets)
```

### Step 5: Readability Optimization

- **Paragraph length:** 2-4 sentences max
- **Sentence length:** 15-20 words average
- **Use transition words:** "however," "moreover," "for example"
- **Add subheadings** every 200-300 words
- **Bold key points** for scanners
- **Use numbered lists** for sequential steps
- **Include images/charts** every 300-500 words

### Step 6: Internal & External Linking

- **Internal links:** 3-5 per post, linking to related content
- **External links:** 2-3 per post, linking to authoritative sources
- **Anchor text:** Use descriptive, keyword-rich phrases

## Power Words and Phrases

### Emotional Triggers
| Category | Words |
|----------|-------|
| Urgency | Now, today, immediately, before it's too late, limited time |
| Curiosity | Secret, hidden, surprising, little-known, revealed |
| Authority | Proven, research-backed, expert-approved, data-driven |
| Exclusivity | Insider, exclusive, members-only, behind-the-scenes |
| Value | Free, bonus, ultimate, comprehensive, complete |

- "Here's the thing..."
- "But wait, there's more..."
- "The real question is..."
- "Let me break this down..."

- "Let's dive in."

### CTA Phrases
- "Start implementing today."
- "Download the free template."
- "Try it yourself — you'll see the difference."
- "What's your experience? Drop a comment below."

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Keyword Stuffing
❌ "If you want to learn SEO writing tips, our SEO writing tips will help you
master SEO writing tips for better SEO writing."

✅ "Looking to improve your search rankings? These writing techniques will help
your content rank higher — no black-hat tricks required."

### 2. Writing for Robots, Not Humans
❌ Long walls of text with no formatting, no personality, and no examples.

✅ Conversational tone, short paragraphs, real examples, and actionable advice.

### 3. Weak Introductions
❌ "In this article, we will discuss the topic of content marketing."

✅ "Last year, we published 52 blog posts. Only 3 of them drove 80% of our
traffic. Here's what those 3 had in common."

### 4. Ignoring Search Intent
❌ Writing a sales page when the keyword is informational ("what is X")

✅ Match the format to what's already ranking in the top 10.

### 5. No Internal Linking
❌ A standalone post with zero links to other content.

✅ 3-5 contextual internal links that help readers explore related topics.

### 6. Neglecting the Meta Description
❌ Leaving it blank or letting Google auto-generate it.

✅ Writing a compelling 150-160 character pitch that includes your keyword.

## Before / After Examples

### Example 1: Introduction

**Before (Weak):**
> Content marketing is important for businesses. It helps you reach more
> customers. In this article, we will talk about content marketing strategies.

**After (Strong):**
> We spent $50,000 on content marketing last year. Here's the uncomfortable
> truth: 90% of it drove zero sales. But the remaining 10%? It generated
> $340,000 in revenue. In this guide, I'll show you exactly how to identify
> and replicate that winning 10%.

### Example 2: Subheading

**Before (Vague):**
> ## Tips for Better Writing

**After (Specific & Keyword-Rich):**
> ## 7 SEO Writing Techniques That Doubled Our Organic Traffic

### Example 3: Body Paragraph

**Before (Wall of Text):**
> Search engine optimization is the process of optimizing your website to rank
> higher in search engine results pages. There are many factors that affect
> rankings including keywords content quality backlinks and technical SEO.
> You should focus on all of these factors to improve your rankings.

**After (Formatted & Scannable):**
> SEO isn't just about keywords anymore. Google now evaluates your content
> across **four key dimensions:**
>
> 1. **Content quality** — Is it comprehensive and accurate?
> 2. **Search intent** — Does it match what the user is looking for?
> 3. **User experience** — Is it easy to read and navigate?
> 4. **Authority signals** — Do other sites link to and reference it?
>
> Nail all four, and you'll see rankings climb within 3-6 months.

### Example 4: CTA

**Before (Generic):**
> Thanks for reading. Please share this article.

**After (Specific & Compelling):**
> You've got the framework. Now it's time to put it to work.
>
> **Your first step:** Pick one of the 7 techniques above and apply it to
> your next blog post. Then come back and tell me how it went in the comments.
