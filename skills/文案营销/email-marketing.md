# Email Marketing Writing

> Write marketing emails that get opened, read, and clicked.

## When to Use

- Building a welcome email sequence for new subscribers
- Writing promotional or sales emails
- Creating abandoned cart recovery emails
- Re-engaging inactive subscribers
- Sending product launch announcements
- Designing email nurture sequences

---

## Writing Framework

### Step 1: Subject Line Formulas

The subject line accounts for 50%+ of your email's success.

| Formula | Example | Best For |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Curiosity gap | "I made a mistake you'll want to avoid" | Newsletters |
| Benefit-driven | "Write 2x faster with this template" | Promotional |
| Urgency | "Last 4 hours: [offer]" | Sales/carts |
| Personal | "[Name], I picked this for you" | E-commerce |
| Question | "Are you making this $10K mistake?" | Educational |
| Number | "3 changes that tripled our open rates" | Content |
| How-to | "How to land your first 1,000 subscribers" | Nurture |
| Social proof | "Why 50,000 marketers read this every week" | Newsletters |

**Subject Line Rules:**
- 30-50 characters (mobile-first)
- Front-load the value
- Avoid spam triggers: "FREE", "Buy now", "$$$", excessive punctuation
- A/B test everything

### Step 2: Preview Text (Preheader)

Preview text is your subject line's partner — 40-100 characters visible before opening.

```
Subject: I made a mistake you'll want to avoid
Preview: And it cost me $3,000. Here's the full story.

Subject: Write 2x faster with this template
Preview: Used by writers at Google, HubSpot, and Shopify.
```

**Rules:**
- Don't repeat the subject line
- Add NEW information or context
- End with an ellipsis (...) to create curiosity

### Step 3: Email Body Structure

```
SUBJECT LINE
PREVIEW TEXT
│
├── FIRST LINE (Hook)
│   ├── Must grab attention in 2 seconds
│   ├── Personal, specific, or surprising
│   └── NO "Hi, I hope you're doing well"
│
├── BODY (Value Delivery)
│   ├── Keep paragraphs to 1-2 sentences
│   ├── Use bold for key points
│   ├── One idea per paragraph
│   ├── White space is your friend
│   └── Total: 150-300 words for promotional, up to 500 for newsletters
│
├── CTA (Call to Action)
│   ├── One primary CTA per email
│   ├── Use action verbs
│   ├── Make it a button (for HTML emails)
│   └── Tell them what happens next
│
└── SIGN-OFF
    ├── Keep it warm and personal
    └── P.S. — Repeat CTA or add urgency
```

### Step 4: CTA Design Principles

| Element | Best Practice |
|---------|---------------|
| Button text | Action verb + benefit ("Get the Template" not "Click Here") |
| Placement | After value delivery, above the fold if possible |
| Color | High contrast, consistent with brand |
| Frequency | 1 primary CTA + max 2 secondary links |
| Mobile | Minimum 44px tap target |

### Step 5: Segmentation & Personalization

| Level | What to Personalize | Impact |
|-------|---------------------|--------|
| Basic | Name, company | +10% open rate |
| Behavioral | Past purchases, page views | +25% click rate |
| Lifecycle | New subscriber vs. loyal customer | +40% conversion |
| Content | Topic preferences | +30% engagement |

## Power Words and Phrases

### Subject Line Power Words
| Category | Words |
|----------|-------|
| Curiosity | secret, surprising, unexpected, behind-the-scenes, revealed |
| Urgency | last chance, ending soon, final hours, don't miss, expires |
| Value | free, exclusive, proven, new, instant |
| Personal | you, your, I, we, [first name] |
| Action | get, try, start, join, discover |

### Opening Lines (After Greeting)
- "I'll keep this short."
- "I've got something special for you."
- "Can I be honest with you?"
- "This is the email I wish someone sent me."
- "I debated whether to send this. Then I decided you need to see it."
- "Quick question..."

### Transition Phrases
- "Here's the thing..."
- "But there's a catch..."
- "Now, here's where it gets interesting..."
- "The real question is..."
- "Which brings me to..."

- "Get instant access →"
- "Start your free trial →"
- "Claim your spot →"
- "Download the template →"
- "See what's inside →"
- "Join [X] others →"

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Writing Like a Corporation
❌ "Dear Valued Customer, We are pleased to inform you of our latest offering."

✅ "Hey [Name], I found something that might help with [their problem]."

### 2. Burying the CTA
❌ CTA hidden at the bottom after 800 words of text.

✅ Place CTA after the value is clear. Make it unmissable.

### 3. No Mobile Optimization
❌ Long paragraphs, small links, wide images.

✅ Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences), large buttons, single-column layout.

### 4. Sending Without Testing
❌ One typo or broken link kills credibility.

✅ Always send a test email. Check: links, images, mobile view, personalization tokens.

### 5. One-Size-Fits-All
❌ Sending the same email to every subscriber.

✅ Segment by: behavior, lifecycle stage, interests, engagement level.

### 6. No Unsubscribe Option
❌ Hiding or removing the unsubscribe link.

✅ Make it easy to unsubscribe. It's better than being marked as spam.

## 中文版本

### 使用场景

- 为新订阅者构建欢迎邮件序列
- 撰写促销或销售邮件
- 创建购物车弃单挽回邮件
- 重新激活不活跃订阅者
- 发送产品发布通知
- 设计邮件培育序列

### 写作框架

**第一步：标题公式** — 好奇心缺口、利益驱动、紧迫感、个性化、问句、数字、How to、社会证明。30-50字符，前置价值，避免垃圾邮件触发词，坚持A/B测试。

**第二步：预览文本** — 40-100字符，不重复标题，补充新信息，用省略号制造悬念。

**第三步：邮件正文结构** — 第一行（钩子，2秒内抓住注意力）→ 正文（1-2句一段，加粗要点，促销邮件150-300词）→ CTA（行动动词+按钮）→ 签名（温暖个人化）→ P.S.（重复CTA或加紧迫感）。

**第四步：CTA设计** — 动词+利益（"获取模板"而非"点击这里"），放在价值传递之后，高对比色，移动端最小44px点击区。

**第五步：分层个性化** — 基础（姓名，+10%打开率）→ 行为（购买历史，+25%点击率）→ 生命周期（新用户vs老客户，+40%转化率）→ 内容偏好（+30%互动率）。

### 模板说明

- **欢迎序列（5封）**：欢迎+资源 → 你的故事 → 快速胜利技巧 → 社会证明 → 产品推荐
- **弃单挽回序列（3封）**：温柔提醒（1小时后）→ 克服异议（24小时后）→ 紧迫感+优惠（48-72小时后）
- **重新激活序列（3封）**：想你了+近况更新 → 精选最佳内容 → 最后告别（48小时后移除）

### 常见错误

1. **写得像企业公告** — "尊敬的客户"不如"嘿[名字]，我发现一个可能帮到你的东西"
2. **CTA埋得太深** — 放在价值传递之后，让人一眼看到
3. **未优化移动端** — 短段落、大按钮、单列布局
4. **未测试就发送** — 发前检查链接、图片、移动端显示、个性化变量
5. **一封邮件群发所有人** — 按行为、生命周期、兴趣分群发送
6. **隐藏退订链接** — 让退订更容易，比被标记垃圾邮件好
