# Social Media Copy Writing

> Write platform-specific social media content that gets engagement, shares, and clicks.

## When to Use

- Creating content for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, or other platforms
- Writing a thread that goes viral
- Crafting posts that drive website traffic
- Building personal brand through content
- Repurposing long-form content for social
- Launching products or announcements on social

---

## Writing Framework

### Step 1: Platform-Specific Formats

Each platform has its own rules. Write accordingly.

| Platform | Ideal Length | Best Formats | Peak Times |
|----------|-------------|--------------|------------|
| Twitter/X | 100-280 chars (threads: 5-15 tweets) | Threads, hot takes, tips | 8-10am, 12-1pm |
| LinkedIn | 1,200-1,500 chars | Stories, lessons, data | Tue-Thu, 7-8am |
| Instagram | 125-150 chars (captions up to 2,200) | Carousels, single image, Reels | 11am-1pm, 7-9pm |
| TikTok | Hook in first 1-2 seconds | Educational, storytelling | 7-9pm |

### Step 2: The Engagement Hook Formula

Your first line determines if they read the rest.

| Hook Type | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Contrarian | "Unpopular opinion: [common practice] is a waste of time." |
| Data-driven | "[Shocking stat] — and here's why it matters." |
| Personal story | "I got fired in 2019. Best thing that ever happened to me." |
| Pattern interrupt | "Stop doing [common thing]. Seriously." |
| Curiosity gap | "I discovered something that changed how I write forever." |
| Direct address | "If you're a [specific persona], read this." |
| List promise | "[Number] things I wish I knew about [topic]:" |

### Step 3: Content Pillars

Rotate between these content types:

| Pillar | Purpose | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Educational (40%) | Teach something valuable | Tips, frameworks, how-tos |
| Personal (25%) | Build connection | Stories, lessons, behind-the-scenes |
| Curated (15%) | Add perspective | Commentary on trends, news, others' content |
| Promotional (10%) | Drive action | Product launches, offers, CTAs |
| Engagement (10%) | Build community | Questions, polls, conversations |

### Step 4: Hashtag Strategy

| Platform | # of Hashtags | Strategy |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Twitter/X | 1-2 max | Only if relevant, often none |
| LinkedIn | 3-5 | Mix of broad and niche |
| Instagram | 5-15 | Mix of sizes: small (<100K), medium (100K-1M), large (>1M) |
| TikTok | 3-5 | Trending + niche |

**Hashtag formula:**
- 2 broad industry hashtags (#marketing, #writing)
- 2-3 niche hashtags (#copywritingtips, #contentstrategy)
- 1-2 branded/campaign hashtags (#YourBrandName)

### Step 5: CTA Strategies by Goal

| Goal | CTA Type | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Engagement | Ask a question | "What's your take? Comment below." |
| Shares | "Tag someone" | "Tag someone who needs to hear this." |
| Traffic | Link in bio/first comment | "Full guide → link in bio" |
| Saves | Resource promise | "Save this for later." |
| Follows | Content promise | "Follow for daily [topic] tips." |

## Power Words and Phrases

### Engagement Hooks by Platform

**Twitter/X:**
- "Hot take:"
- "Unpopular opinion:"
- "Here's what nobody tells you about [topic]:"
- "I analyzed [X]. Here's what I found:"
- "Stop [doing common thing]."
- "[Number] things I learned from [experience]:"

**LinkedIn:**
- "I made a huge mistake."
- "[Number] years ago, I [experience]."
- "Here's the truth about [topic] that nobody talks about."
- "I interviewed [number]+ [people]. The #1 thing they all said:"
- "I was wrong about [topic]. Here's what changed my mind."

**Instagram:**
- "POV: [scenario]"
- "This is your sign to [action]"
- "Tell me [X] without telling me [X]"
- "The [topic] guide you didn't know you needed"
- "Save this for when you need it"

### Power Phrases for Engagement
| Category | Phrases |
|----------|---------|
| Curiosity | "Here's the thing...", "The truth is...", "What surprised me..." |
| Authority | "After [X] years...", "Having worked with [X]...", "Data shows..." |
| Relatability | "Can anyone else relate?", "We've all been there", "Real talk:" |
| Urgency | "Before it's too late", "Right now", "This week" |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Writing the Same Content for Every Platform
❌ Posting a tweet-length caption on LinkedIn, or a LinkedIn essay on Twitter.

✅ Adapt length, tone, and format to each platform's culture.

### 2. No Hook in the First Line
❌ Starting with "So I was thinking about marketing the other day..."

✅ "Your marketing strategy has a $50,000 blind spot. Here's what it is."

### 3. All Talk, No Engagement
❌ Broadcasting without inviting conversation.

✅ End with a question. Reply to every comment in the first hour.

### 4. Ignoring Formatting
❌ Dense paragraphs with no line breaks on LinkedIn or Instagram.

✅ Use line breaks, bullet points, and white space generously.

### 5. Being Generic
❌ "Content is king. Consistency is key. Keep posting!"

✅ Share specific lessons, real numbers, named examples, and honest opinions.

### 6. No Clear CTA
❌ Just ending abruptly without telling people what to do next.

✅ "Follow for more" / "Save this" / "Comment your experience" / "Link in bio"

## 中文版本

### 使用场景

- 为 Twitter/X、LinkedIn、Instagram 等平台创建内容
- 撰写病毒式传播的 thread
- 制作驱动网站流量的帖子
- 通过内容打造个人品牌
- 将长内容改编为社交媒体帖子
- 在社交平台发布产品或公告

### 写作框架

**第一步：平台差异化** — Twitter/X：100-280字符，适合线程和热点观点；LinkedIn：1200-1500字符，适合故事和经验；Instagram：125-150字符（配文），适合轮播和短视频；TikTok：前1-2秒必须抓住注意力。

**第二步：钩子公式** — 反常识观点、数据驱动、个人故事、模式中断、好奇心缺口、直接对话、清单承诺。

**第三步：内容支柱** — 教育类（40%）+ 个人类（25%）+ 精选类（15%）+ 推广类（10%）+ 互动类（10%）。

**第四步：话题标签策略** — Twitter 1-2个、LinkedIn 3-5个、Instagram 5-15个、TikTok 3-5个。组合：2个行业大标签 + 2-3个细分标签 + 1-2个品牌标签。

**第五步：CTA策略** — 互动目标用提问、转发目标用"@好友"、流量目标用"链接在简介"、收藏目标用"收藏备用"。

### 模板说明

- **Twitter/X 线程模板**：钩子推文 → 背景 → 5个要点（每点含解释+数据）→ 总结 → CTA
- **LinkedIn 帖子模板**：三种格式 — 故事式（场景→冲突→转折→教训）、清单式（编号技巧）、反常识式（观点+论据）
- **Instagram 配文模板**：单图帖（前125字符关键 + 3-5段 + CTA + 标签）、轮播帖（每页一个要点）

### 常见错误

1. **所有平台发同样内容** — 应适配每个平台的长度、语气和格式
2. **首行无钩子** — 第一行决定是否继续阅读
3. **只广播不互动** — 结尾提问，前一小时回复每条评论
4. **忽视格式** — LinkedIn 和 Instagram 要善用换行和留白
5. **内容泛泛** — 分享具体教训、真实数据、诚实观点
6. **无明确CTA** — 告诉读者下一步做什么：关注/收藏/评论/点链接
