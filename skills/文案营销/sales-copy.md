# Sales Copy Writing

> Write persuasive sales copy that converts visitors into customers.

## When to Use

- Writing landing page copy
- Creating product descriptions
- Developing email sales sequences
- Crafting ad copy for paid campaigns
- Writing sales pages for digital products
- Creating upsell and cross-sell copy

---

## Writing Framework

### Step 1: Know Your Customer

Before writing a word, answer these questions:

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| What problem keeps them up at night? | This is your hook |
| What have they already tried? | This is what you're replacing |
| What's their biggest fear about buying? | This is your objection to handle |
| What language do they use? | This is how you write |
| What's the #1 outcome they want? | This is your headline |

### Step 2: Choose Your Framework

#### AIDA Framework (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)

```
1. ATTENTION — Stop the scroll
   → Bold claim, shocking stat, or provocative question

2. INTEREST — Make them lean in
   → Tell them WHY this matters to THEM

3. DESIRE — Make them want it
   → Paint the transformation, show proof

4. ACTION — Tell them what to do
   → Clear, specific, urgent CTA
```

#### PAS Framework (Problem → Agitation → Solution)

```
1. PROBLEM — Name their pain
   → "You're spending hours on [task] and still not getting results."

2. AGITATION — Twist the knife
   → "Every day you wait, you're losing [money/time/opportunities].
      Your competitors aren't waiting."

3. SOLUTION — Present the way out
   → "[Product] eliminates [problem] in [timeframe]. Here's how."
```

### Step 3: Feature-Benefit Conversion

Every feature must be converted to a benefit:

```
Feature: What it IS
Benefit: What it DOES FOR THEM

Formula: "[Feature] so that [benefit]"

Examples:
Feature: "5GB storage" → Benefit: "Store 10,000 photos without worrying about space"
Feature: "24/7 support" → Benefit: "Get help at 2am when your site goes down"
Feature: "AI-powered" → Benefit: "Save 5 hours a week on repetitive tasks"
```

### Step 4: Objection Handling

Address objections BEFORE they arise:

| Common Objection | How to Handle It |
|------------------|-----------------|
| "It's too expensive" | Show ROI, cost comparison, or payment plans |
| "I don't have time" | Show how it SAVES time, with specific numbers |
| "It won't work for me" | Show diverse case studies and use cases |
| "I need to think about it" | Create urgency (limited time, bonus expiring) |
| "What if I don't like it?" | Money-back guarantee, free trial |
| "I've tried something similar" | Explain what's different and why it matters |

### Step 5: Power Structure for Sales Pages

```
1. HEADLINE — [Big promise + specific benefit]
2. SUB-HEADLINE — [Expand on who it's for and what they get]
3. OPENING STORY — [Relatable pain point, 2-3 paragraphs]
4. PROBLEM SECTION — [List all the frustrations they face]
5. SOLUTION INTRO — [Introduce your product as the answer]
6. BENEFITS SECTION — [3-5 key benefits with proof]
7. SOCIAL PROOF — [Testimonials, numbers, logos]
8. HOW IT WORKS — [Simple 3-step process]
9. OBJECTION HANDLING — [Address top 3-5 objections]
10. PRICING — [Clear pricing with value stack]
11. GUARANTEE — [Risk reversal]
12. FINAL CTA — [Urgent, clear, specific]
13. P.S. — [Repeat main benefit + urgency]
```

## Power Words and Phrases

### Conversion Power Words
| Category | Words |
|----------|-------|
| Value | Free, bonus, exclusive, premium, instant |
| Trust | Guaranteed, proven, certified, tested, endorsed |
| Urgency | Now, today, limited, last chance, expires |
| Simplicity | Easy, simple, effortless, quick, step-by-step |
| Exclusivity | Members-only, insider, secret, invitation, VIP |
| Results | Transform, double, boost, skyrocket, eliminate |

### Benefit Phrases
- "So you can finally..."
- "Without having to..."
- "Even if you've never..."
- "In just [timeframe]..."
- "While you sleep/relax/focus on..."
- "Imagine [desired outcome]..."

### Objection-Handling Phrases
- "I know what you're thinking..."
- "You might be wondering..."
- "Here's why that's not a problem..."
- "But what about...?"
- "The #1 question I get asked is..."

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Writing About Features, Not Benefits
❌ "Our tool has AI-powered analytics with real-time dashboards."

✅ "See exactly what's working — and what's not — in real time.
No more guessing which campaigns drive revenue."

### 2. Being Vague
❌ "Increase your productivity."

✅ "Write 3x more content in half the time. Our users publish an average
of 12 blog posts per month, up from 4."

### 3. No Social Proof
❌ "We're the best solution on the market."

✅ "Join 10,000+ marketers who increased their conversion rate by an
average of 34% in the first 90 days."

### 4. Weak CTA
❌ "Click here" or "Learn more"

✅ "Start Your Free Trial — No Credit Card Required"
or "Get the Template Pack (Instant Download)"

### 5. Not Addressing Objections
❌ Hoping they won't notice the price or the risks.

✅ Proactively addressing "too expensive," "too complicated," and
"what if it doesn't work" with specific responses.

### 6. Writing for Everyone
❌ "This product is great for anyone who wants to be more productive."

✅ "Built for content marketers who publish 4+ times per week and need
to cut production time without sacrificing quality."
